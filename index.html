Sustainability and Civil Engagement

Solidarity trip in Bolivia

Two years ago, I went on a volunteer trip to Bolivia. The goal of this community project was to meet the local populations and support them financially in building a school in a small village deep in the Amazon rainforest. Thanks to this solidarity trip, I was able to spend three weeks completely disconnected — without any network — close to nature and a simple life.

Project about Space debris and sustainable space

In our second year of preparatory school, we carried out a project with five classmates on space debris in order to assess its impact on our world. To do this, we visited CNES and Supaero to interview engineers and researchers working on space debris. Through this project, we became aware of the current challenges in the space industry, learned about methods for recovering certain debris, and discovered initiatives aimed at making space more sustainable and reducing emissions!

Sports and other activities

Cheerleading Experience 🤸‍♀️
I have been practicing high-level cheerleading for three years with the Wolves Toulouse Cheerleading team.
Often mistaken for pom-pom dancing, cheerleading is actually a demanding acrobatic sport combining gymnastics, stunts, and pyramids.
I regularly take part in both national and international competitions, such as the World Championships, where my team and I were crowned World Champions in 2025 (INTERNATIONAL CHEERLEADING CUP).

Because of this commitment, cheerleading now requires a significant investment of time — around 10 hours of training per week.
This sport has brought me countless benefits, both personally and professionally. It has helped me develop key skills such as:

  • Determination and discipline: consistently attending all mandatory training sessions and striving to improve.
  • Stress management: performing under pressure during major national and international competitions where every routine counts.
  • Organization and time management: balancing a demanding preparatory program with intensive training sessions.
  • Team spirit and adaptability: working within a 24-member team requires learning to collaborate effectively, adjust to different personalities, and build strong group cohesion.

In short, cheerleading has truly transformed my life over the past three years. It has shaped who I am today and strengthened my motivation to pursue new goals and challenges — such as taking part in the French national team selections — and to keep progressing every day.
